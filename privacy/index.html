Privacy

Musubi: IVF Treatment Notebook · Effective 8 November 2026

Your treatment records never leave your iPhone. Musubi has no account and keeps no copy of your records on any server. Two things can leave your iPhone, and neither contains your records: anonymous usage statistics, only if you agree, and a record of a Musubi Pro purchase, so we can see our sales. No ads, no tracking and no sale of data.

Who is responsible

Musubi is made by edevzi, an independent developer (“we”). We are responsible for the data described on this page. Contact: edevzime@gmail.com.

Your treatment records

Everything you enter — medications, doses, trigger time, cycle dates, retrieval results, blood test values, visits, costs, receipt photos and notes — is stored only on your iPhone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and Musubi does not use iCloud sync. Widgets and the Lock Screen countdown read a small summary that stays on the same iPhone.

If you back up your iPhone (for example with iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup), Apple includes app data in that backup under Apple’s own privacy terms. We cannot access it.

When you share or export something yourself (a message to your partner, a PDF summary or a spreadsheet), it goes only where you send it with the iOS share sheet.

Optional anonymous usage statistics

At the end of setup Musubi asks whether you want to share anonymous usage statistics. They are off unless you choose “Share anonymous usage”. Nothing is sent before you choose, and nothing is sent if you choose “Don’t share”. You can change your choice at any time in Musubi’s Settings. Your choice does not change any feature or price.

If you turn them on, Musubi sends the following to our server at musubi.edevzi.uz:

  • a random install ID that Musubi creates on your iPhone (not your Apple Account, not the advertising identifier);
  • app version and build, iOS version, iPhone model, app language, App Store country, the region and currency set on your iPhone, and your time-zone offset;
  • whether you have Musubi Pro, and which plan;
  • which screens and buttons are used: for example opening the Today or Costs screen, finishing setup, viewing the Pro page, starting or completing a purchase (with plan, price and currency), restoring purchases, tapping an export, and whether a widget, the Lock Screen countdown or an alternate icon is in use.

The statistics never include medication names, doses, dates, trigger times, lab values, costs, notes, receipt photos or anything else you type. They are not linked to your name, email address or Apple Account. We do not store your IP address or any location derived from it.

Why we use them

Only to improve Musubi: to see which features help, where people get stuck, whether reminders, widgets and purchases work on each iOS version and iPhone model, and which languages and countries to support. We do not use them for advertising, we do not combine them with data from other companies, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we never sell or share them. Our legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Who processes them

Purchases (Musubi Pro)

Payments are made and processed by Apple; we never see your card or Apple Account details. When you buy or restore Musubi Pro, the app also sends the purchase record (which plan, price and currency, purchase and renewal dates, and the App Store's transaction identifiers) to RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States), the service we use to count sales, renewals and refunds. It is filed under a random identifier created on your iPhone, not your name, email or Apple Account, and is never combined with your treatment records, which stay on your iPhone. RevenueCat processes it only on our behalf under its data processing terms. This happens only when you make a purchase or tap Restore.

The server runs on Vercel (Vercel Inc., United States). The statistics are stored with Upstash (Redis) and Neon (Postgres), which Vercel provides as its subprocessors, in the United States. These providers process the data only on our behalf, under their data processing terms, and must protect it at least as well as described here. No one else receives it.

How long we keep them

We keep raw usage events for at most 24 months, then delete them. Turning the statistics off deletes your install’s data sooner (see below).

Turning them off and deleting them

In Musubi’s Settings, turn off “Share anonymous usage statistics”. Musubi stops sending at once and asks our server to delete everything stored for your install ID; the deletion is normally completed within a day. Because the statistics are anonymous, we cannot find them from your name or email address, so this switch is the way to delete them. If you delete Musubi while sharing is on, the remaining statistics cannot be linked to you and are deleted when the 24-month period ends.

Update check

Once in a while Musubi asks Apple’s public App Store lookup service whether a newer version exists. The request goes to Apple, not to us, and contains only Musubi’s app identifier and your App Store country.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details, name or email address. Musubi checks your Pro status with Apple on your iPhone.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications created on your iPhone. By default they do not show medication names.

Deleting your records

Settings → Delete all data removes every record, reminder and exported file at once. Deleting the app also deletes its data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK, Japan or California), you may have the right to access or delete your data, to withdraw your consent and to complain to a data protection authority. We hold no treatment records and the statistics are anonymous, so the quickest way to exercise these rights is the Settings switch above. You can also write to us.

Children

Musubi is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Medical information

Musubi is a notebook, not medical advice. It does not calculate doses or make diagnoses. Always follow your clinic’s instructions.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and Musubi will ask for your consent before any new kind of data is sent. Questions: edevzime@gmail.com
