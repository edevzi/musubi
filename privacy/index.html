Privacy

Musubi: IVF Tracker & Shots · Effective 8 November 2026

Musubi collects no data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK.

Where your records are

Everything you enter — medications, doses, trigger time, cycle dates, retrieval results, blood test values, visits, costs and receipt photos — is stored only on your iPhone. It is not uploaded to us, and Musubi does not use iCloud sync. Widgets and the Lock Screen countdown read a small summary that stays on the same iPhone.

If you back up your iPhone (for example with iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup), Apple includes app data in that backup under Apple’s own privacy terms. We cannot access it.

Network use

  • Once in a while Musubi asks Apple’s public App Store lookup service whether a newer version exists. The request contains only the app’s identifier and your App Store country.
  • Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications created on your iPhone. By default they do not show medication names.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes every record, reminder and exported file at once. Deleting the app also deletes its data.

Children

Musubi is not directed to children.

Medical information

Musubi does not give medical advice and does not calculate doses. Always follow your clinic’s instructions.
