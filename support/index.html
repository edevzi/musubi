Are my records safe?

Your treatment records never leave your iPhone. There is no account and no copy on a server. See the privacy policy.

Usage statistics: on or off

At the end of setup Musubi asks whether you want to share anonymous usage statistics. They stay off unless you choose “Share anonymous usage”. To change it later, open Musubi’s Settings and switch “Share anonymous usage statistics” on or off. Turning it off stops sending and deletes your install’s statistics from our server. The statistics never include medication names, doses, dates, lab values, costs or notes.

How reminders work

Reminders are local notifications that Musubi schedules on your iPhone: one for every dose, and for the trigger one hour before and at the time. Musubi refreshes the upcoming reminders in the background, so you don’t need to open it every week. After the last scheduled reminder it adds one more, “Open Musubi to keep your reminders coming.”, so you know when it needs a moment to set the next ones.

Reminders don’t arrive

Allow notifications for Musubi in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Musubi, and check that a Focus isn’t silencing them. Then open Musubi once so the upcoming reminders are scheduled again.

What is free and what is Pro?

Free: medications and doses, all reminders, the trigger screen, cycle milestones, blood test values, retrieval results, earlier cycles and widgets. Musubi Pro adds the trigger countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, blood test trend charts, the costs and receipts ledger with a yearly export, a PDF summary for your clinic, partner sharing and alternate app icons.

New iPhone?

Move everything with Quick Start or by restoring an iPhone backup. Musubi’s data travels inside the backup.

I bought Pro but it isn’t active

Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Musubi Pro. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period.

Musubi is a notebook, not medical advice. Always follow your clinic’s instructions.

日本語

記録は安全？ 治療の記録はiPhoneの外に出ることはありません。アカウントもサーバー上のコピーもありません。

利用統計のオン・オフ： 初回設定の最後に、匿名の利用統計を共有するかどうかをお尋ねします。「共有する」を選ばない限りオフのままです。あとから変更するには、Musubiの設定で「匿名の利用統計を共有」を切り替えてください。オフにすると送信が止まり、サーバー上のそのインストールのデータが削除されます。

リマインダーのしくみ： すべての薬と、トリガーの1時間前と時刻ちょうどに、iPhoneの中で通知を予約します。予定のリマインダーはバックグラウンドで更新されるので、毎週アプリを開く必要はありません。予約された最後のリマインダーのあとに「Musubiを開いてください」という通知を1件追加します。届かないときは、iPhoneの「設定」→「通知」→「Musubi」で通知を許可し、集中モードを確認してから、Musubiを一度開いてください。
