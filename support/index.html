Are my records safe?

They never leave your iPhone. There is no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

New iPhone?

Move everything with Quick Start or by restoring an iPhone backup. Musubi’s data travels inside the backup.

I bought Pro but it isn’t active

Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Musubi Pro. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period.

Reminders don’t arrive

Allow notifications for Musubi in iPhone Settings, and open Musubi at least once a week so the next reminders are scheduled.

Musubi does not give medical advice. Always follow your clinic’s instructions.
